Bluffton Golf Rewards / loyalty & direct communication

More than a pass. A reason to return.

A mobile wallet pass puts a useful piece of the club relationship where it belongs: in the customer’s phone. It makes rewards easy to access at the moment of play—and creates a permissioned channel for relevant follow-up.

The bigger picture: when a business is quieter, direct SMS, email, and push communication can bring a past customer back with the right offer, reminder, or reason to visit. The pass connects those touchpoints to the relationship—not just a one-time transaction.

1 / Earn & recognizeGive regular play a clear, useful value exchange.
2 / Stay connectedUse SMS, email, and push messages for timely reminders and offers.
3 / Bring people backSupport repeat visits when the business needs demand.
