Bluffton Golf Club / SEO, AEO & conversion-led website

Get found. Be the answer. Earn the booking.

This was never a website makeover. I started with SEO/AEO audits, a revenue-visibility study, and a six-course comparative analysis to find where the legacy site was making it harder for the right golfer to discover the club, understand it, and take action.

01 / Get discovered with SEO

Dedicated, descriptive pages for tee times, membership, lessons, outings, and local golf searches give search engines a clear reason to surface the club when intent is high.

02 / Be understood by AI with AEO

Consistent facts, direct answers, FAQs, and structured information make the official site easier for AI search, voice tools, and answer engines to recognize, explain, and cite accurately.

03 / Turn discovery into revenue

Every high-value question now leads somewhere useful: book a tee time, explore membership, request a lesson, plan an outing, or contact the club—without sending the customer into a dead end.

Why the comparative work mattered: the goal was not to copy another course. It was to identify what search and answer engines reward—clear site structure, local relevance, useful content, accurate entity facts, and strong paths to action—then build those advantages into Bluffton’s own digital foundation.

The launch plan matters too: the new site is the source of truth, while 301 redirects, listing/citation updates, reviews, and ongoing local content help transfer that on-site strength into long-term search authority.
